> Airtable: Software Engineer, Compute (8+ YOE)



<p>
  <strong>Headquarters:</strong> San Francisco, CA; New York, NY; Remote - US
</p>

<div class="content-intro"><p>Airtable is the no-code app platform that empowers people closest to the work to accelerate their most critical business processes. More than 500,000 organizations, including 80% of the Fortune 100, rely on Airtable to transform how work gets done.</p></div><p>The Compute Platform team is responsible for building and evolving the infrastructure that powers Airtable’s services at scale. We enable engineers across the company to build, deploy, and operate reliable services on Kubernetes with strong security, governance, and performance guardrails.</p>
<p>We are looking for an engineer to help lead the next phase of platform maturity in how we run Kubernetes at Airtable. This is a role that has engineering wide impact. If you’re excited about building internal platforms, shaping infrastructure strategy, and partnering closely with product and security teams to power a rapidly growing enterprise business, we’d love to hear from you.</p>
<h1><strong>What You’ll Do:</strong></h1>
<h3><strong>Build and evolve Airtable’s compute platform</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Design, implement, and scale core Kubernetes platform capabilities used across ~70 clusters, spread across multiple environments.</li>
<li>Lead foundational modernization efforts such as migrating to a new CNI plugin to overhaul IP security rule management across clusters and regions</li>
<li>Rewrite our core deployment service from Typescript to Golang and adopt performant Kubernetes operator patterns to eliminate scaling bottlenecks on our larger clusters</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Raise the bar on platform maturity</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Improve developer experience around new service creation, deployment workflows, and operational best practices</li>
<li>Partner with Security to implement governance controls, least privilege access, image signing, and enterprise-grade infrastructure safeguards</li>
<li>Define and uphold operational excellence standards across the platform by defining good SLOs, measuring and upholding them</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Lead through technical influence</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Spend roughly 70% of your time in hands-on engineering and 30% in design reviews, mentorship, and cross-team collaboration</li>
<li>Review architecture proposals and guide high-impact infrastructure decisions</li>
<li>Mentor engineers on distributed systems and Kubernetes platform design</li>
<li>Collaborate closely with developers building and deploying services across Airtable</li>
</ul>
<h1><strong>Who You Are:</strong></h1>
<ul>
<li>8+ years of software engineering experience, with deep expertise building and operating a Kubernetes based internal service platform,&nbsp;</li>
<li>Strong understanding of Kubernetes internals, including controllers/operators, CRDs, networking, and cluster architecture</li>
<li>Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, OpenTofu, Pulumi, or similar</li>
<li>Experience with CI/CD and deployment tooling such as Spinnaker, ArgoCD, Flux, Jenkins, or similar</li>
<li>Clear, thoughtful communicator who collaborates effectively across many engineering teams</li>
<li>Empathetic partner to product engineers, with a strong sense of ownership and autonomy</li>
<li>Preferably experience with AWS infrastructure and EKS</li>
</ul>
