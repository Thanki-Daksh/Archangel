<div>
<p><strong>About Qualifyze,</strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span>Founded in 2019, Qualifyze is a leading company in supply chain compliance management in the Life Sciences industry, trusted by over 1,500 pharmaceutical and healthcare companies globally. Our digital suite of solutions connects manufacturers, suppliers, and a global network of more than 250 auditors and quality experts.</span></p>
<p><span>With a track record of over 4,500 audits across 85+ countries and the largest and most accurate supplier network and data analytics tools, Qualifyze stands as the all-in-one partner for quality compliance and supply chain risk mitigation in the Life Sciences sector.</span></p>

<h2 class="sc-1npqnwg-4 sc-1npqnwg-5 sc-1uwf3m5-0 sc-1uwf3m5-1 cpVUVQ custom-css-style-job-requirements">Job requirements</h2>
<div class="sc-1fwbcuw-0 dosqqI">
<div>
<p><strong><span>Role Overview, </span></strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span>At Qualifyze, we view infrastructure as a critical component of our overall business strategy. As our Senior DevOps/SRE Engineer, you will play a strategic role within our engineering department, directly reporting to the VP of Engineering and contributing to the departmental strategy from an infrastructure perspective. You will define, implement, and maintain our servers and database infrastructure, ensuring our systems are secure, compliant, and scalable.</span></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><span>Main responsibilities,</span></strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span>This role is integrated into the Tech department. The team member shall perform the tasks inherent to such a position, including, but not limited to, the following:</span></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Define, implement, and maintain cloud-based server</span></strong><span> and database infrastructure, managed with Kubernetes.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>IaC as the base</span></strong><span> philosophy for </span><strong><span>managing infrastructure</span></strong><span>.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Own and manage our monitoring setup</span></strong><span>, utilizing all modules of Datadog for proactive infrastructure monitoring.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Collaborate closely with team lead</span></strong><span>s and the software architect to ensure alignment across our engineering efforts, and be an active part of our tech guild.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Guarantee the security and compliance of our environments</span></strong><span>, working towards achieving necessary certifications and standards.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Contribute to strategic decision-making on infrastructure</span></strong><span> to ensure pragmatic solutions that align with product direction and future growth.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Collaborate with development teams to design, implement, and manage CI/CD</span></strong><span> pipelines that foster fast and safe software delivery.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Mentor other engineers</span></strong><span> on DevOps best practices and provide guidance on infrastructure-related topics.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span>Proactively </span><strong><span>identify opportunities for system improvements</span></strong><span> and drive the implementation of those solutions.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong><span>Main requirements,</span></strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><span>Significant experience with </span><strong><span>cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred)</span></strong><span>, </span><strong><span>Kubernetes</span></strong><span>, </span><strong><span>Helm</span></strong><span>, and tools like </span><strong><span>Datadog</span></strong><span>.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span>Good knowledge of </span><strong><span>managing IaC with Terraform</span></strong><span>.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Experience creating and managing pipelines and workflows,</span></strong><span> with GitHub Actions or similar.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>A</span></strong><span> </span><strong><span>strategic mindset</span></strong><span>, with a willingness to participate in high-level discussions on product and infrastructure strategy.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Ability to work autonomously</span></strong><span>, take ownership of projects, and drive them to completion with minimal oversight.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Strong understanding of security and compliance requirements</span></strong><span>, with experience implementing compliant solutions in a cloud environment.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Experience collaborating with software development teams and technical leadership</span></strong><span>, including architects and team leads.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Effective communication skills</span></strong><span> to explain complex technical concepts to stakeholders across various departments.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Passion for creating pragmatic solutions</span></strong><span> that align with product and business needs.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong><span>Ability to lead initiatives independently</span></strong><span> and provide technical expertise to the engineering team.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
