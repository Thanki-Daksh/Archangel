<p><strong>About the job</strong></p>
<p>As a Principal Engineer on the Twilio Segment Data platform/ pipelines team, you’ll build and scale systems that process several hundred thousands of data points per second. You will lead the development of high-scale ingestion and data processing systems You'll guide the team in designing, operating and maintaining complex distributed systems, ensuring reliability, performance, and cost-efficiency while querying petabytes of data for our customer data platform (CDP).</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p>In this role, you’ll:</p>
<ul>
<li>Own and deliver robust, high-scale routing experiences for the Data platform/ pipelines team for Twilio Segment.</li>
<li>Architect and operate always-available, complex distributed systems in cloud environments.</li>
<li>Guide technical decisions, articulating trade-offs between cost, performance, and reliability.</li>
<li>Translate complex technical strategies into actionable plans for diverse stakeholders, including Product Managers and Architects</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Qualifications&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply. If your career is just starting or hasn't followed a traditional path, don't let that stop you from considering Twilio. We are always looking for people who will bring something new to the table!</p>
<p><strong>*Required:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.</li>
<li>Hands-on experience with high-scale messaging/streaming systems and processing engines (several thousand events/sec).&nbsp;</li>
<li>Excellent abilities in high level service design -- modeling interfaces and internals in a properly modular, composable, and extensible way.</li>
<li>Expert programming experience in Go or similar object-oriented languages.</li>
<li>Demonstrated experience identifying, prototyping, and deploying GenAI/LLM solutions to solve real-world engineering or data processing challenges.</li>
<li>Technical depth in distributed systems, high-scale ingestion, and cloud-native architecture.</li>
<li>Excellent communication, problem-solving, and operational excellence skills.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Desired:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Proficiency with datastores, distributed caching, and container technologies (Docker/Kubernetes).&nbsp;</li>
<li>Experience with modern CI/CD pipelines and observability tools (Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus).</li>
<li>Familiarity with AI/ML technologies.</li>
<li>Experience managing and operating AWS infrastructure</li>
<li>Experience working in a geographically distributed team.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
