<div>&nbsp;</div>
<div>&nbsp;</div>
<div>Corporate &amp; Strategic Programs (CSP) sits within Coinbase's central Business org, leveraging program management to supercharge execution on the company's most critical priorities. As a Manager, Corporate Programs, you'll own two of our most visible cross-functional programs: CORA (Change Oversight &amp; Risk Assessment), which mitigates risk across all product launches and system changes, and Planning, which manages Executive QBRs, OKRs, and dependencies management. You'll drive both programs end-to-end, translating complex process challenges into scalable solutions with direct visibility to senior leadership.<br><strong>What you'll do:</strong></div>
<ul>
<li>Conduct research and analysis to understand internal customer pain points and create solutions prioritized into a clear roadmap</li>
<li>Drive Executive QBR planning cycles end-to-end, from company guidance through to final outputs</li>
<li>Maintain CORA's operating rhythms including weekly and monthly reporting, and represent CORA during audits and compliance exams with internal and external stakeholders including regulators</li>
<li>Partner with technical teams (IT, data, engineering) when solutions require more complex technical builds</li>
<li>Maintain all enablement resources including guidance, policies, procedures, FAQs, training materials, and AI agents</li>
<li>Identify and implement scalable solutions to systemic process and tooling challenges across both programs</li>
</ul>
<div><strong>Required Skills and Experience:</strong></div>
<ul>
<li>6+ years in product operations or program management for software/product initiatives, with experience owning cross-functional programs end-to-end</li>
<li>Demonstrated ability to turn ambiguous or conflicting inputs into clear, actionable plans and drive them to completion</li>
<li>Experience partnering with technical teams and translating business requirements into tooling or process solutions</li>
<li>Proficiency in Atlassian suite (Jira, Confluence) and experience leveraging AI to automate and accelerate workflows</li>
<li>Strong written and verbal communication with the ability to facilitate senior stakeholder dialogues and drive toward outcomes</li>
<li>Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.</li>
</ul>
