<p>This position is needed to manage the <strong>Flex Billing &amp; Backend team</strong> of software engineers to own and extend the feature set offering to customers and therefore the overall solution reach of Flex.</p>
<p>Flex is Twilio’s contact center platform and is a unique solution that provides customers with the building blocks they need to build a fully customized contact center solution to meet their needs.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p>In this role, you’ll:</p>
<ul>

<li>Be a champion for your team, setting individuals up for success and putting others’ growth first.</li>
<li>Understand the architecture and processes required to build and operate always-available complex and scalable distributed systems in cloud environments.</li>
<li>Advocate agile processes, continuous integration and test automation.</li>
<li>Be a strategic problem solver and thrive operating in broad scope, from conception through continuous operation of 24x7 services.</li>
<li>Exhibit strong communication skills: in person, or on paper. You can explain technical concepts to product managers, architects, other engineers, and support.</li>
</ul>
