<p><strong>Senior Independent AI Engineer / Architect</strong></p>
<p><em>Remote | Americas, Europe, or Israel</em></p>

<p>Finding work worth doing is harder.</p>
<p>A.Team is an invite-only network of senior AI engineers, ML engineers, and AI architects working on production AI systems for startups, scale-ups, and global companies.</p>

<p>You'll join small teams tackling problems that matter.</p>
<p>Not demos.</p>
<p>Not prototypes that never ship.</p>
<p>Not another chatbot wrapper.</p>
<p>Real systems. Real users. Real outcomes.</p>

<p>The best opportunities rarely make it to the open market.</p>

<p>No bidding.</p>
