<p>Are you ready to take the lead in shaping the future of logistics technology in North America? Join our client tech team as the Director of DevOps and be a key player in their dynamic, rapidly growing team. <p><strong>Key Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Lead and inspire a DevOps team, driving excellence in project management, tool selection, mentoring, and developing cutting-edge DevOps practices.</li>
<li>Manage complex tasks with minimal guidance, ensuring critical infrastructure components' reliability, adaptability, and resilience.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Challenges You'll Tackle:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Deliver high-quality products by understanding and addressing DevOps needs related to AWS and Azure.</li>
<li>Collaborate closely with the DevOps team to implement specialized tools, distribution pipelines, and processes for continuous integration and delivery.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Your Impact:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Ensure the reliability, adaptability, and resilience of crucial infrastructure components, taking responsibility for servers, databases, hosted services, load balancers, and more.</li>
<li>Actively participate in incident escalation and rapid resolution, constantly improving and fine-tuning systems to guarantee service availability.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Sharing Knowledge and Inspiring Growth:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Actively contribute to our internal peer learning platform, sharing your expertise in various technologies with a class every six months to foster continuous learning.</li>
<li>Lead the onboarding process for new DevOps team members and establish schedules and priorities for their success.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Contributing to Talent Acquisition:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Play a pivotal role in HR recruitment activities, identifying and attracting top-tier DevOps talent to join our innovative team.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Your Profile:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, or a related technical field.</li>
<li>A minimum of 3 years of successful DevOps team management experience.</li>
<li>Proven track record with at least 3 years in managing Linux environments (Red Hat, CentOS, Debian, or Ubuntu).</li>
<li>Minimum 2 years of experience in service-oriented architectures, software development life cycle, and continuous integration and delivery practices.</li>
<li>Proficiency in Git, automation concepts, and tools (Terraform, Ansible).</li>
<li>Knowledgeable about cloud and container concepts.</li>
<li>Exceptional problem-solving skills, a collaborative team spirit, and the ability to thrive in dynamic environments.</li>
<li>Fluent in English; proficiency in French is an asset.</li>
<li><span>Determination and ability to learn quickly</span></li>
<li><span>High work ethic and results-oriented mindset</span></li>
<li><span>A sharp sense of responsibility and leadership</span></li>
<li><span>Dynamic optimism and emphasis on finding solutions to overcome challenges</span></li>
<li><span>Team spirit and a natural ability to build connections</span></li>
<li><span>Ability to thrive in dynamic environments and master rapidly evolving web technologies and techniques</span></li>
