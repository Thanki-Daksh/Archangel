<p><strong>About the job</strong></p>
<p>This position is needed to lead the planning, design, development, and testing of large-scale software systems and new product capabilities, including cloud and internet-facing services. You’ll set technical direction, drive delivery using modern engineering practices, and ensure what we ship is secure, compliant, and production-ready. You’ll partner closely with product and engineering teams to turn requirements into reliable, high-quality software.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p>In this role, you’ll:</p>
<ul>
<li>Lead architecture and system design for complex, distributed software systems; guide teams toward scalable, maintainable solutions.</li>
<li>Design, implement, and review high-quality code for core services, APIs, platforms, and/or user-facing applications.</li>
<li>Translate business goals into technical roadmaps, identify tradeoffs, and align stakeholders on solution direction.</li>
<li>Improve system resiliency, latency, throughput, and cost efficiency; establish SLOs/SLAs and operational practices.</li>
<li>Drive standards for coding, testing, CI/CD, observability, security, and documentation; champion automation and quality.</li>
<li>Partner with engineering managers, staff engineers, and product leaders to coordinate delivery across multiple teams.</li>
<li>Coach and mentor engineers at all levels; lead design reviews, incident postmortems, and technical deep dives.</li>
<li>Identify technical risks early (security, scalability, compliance) and drive mitigation plans.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Qualifications&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>Twilio values diverse experiences from all kinds of industries, and we encourage everyone who meets the required qualifications to apply. If your career is just starting or hasn't followed a traditional path, don't let that stop you from considering Twilio. We are always looking for people who will bring something new to the table!</p>
<p><strong>*Required:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>12+ years of experience designing and developing highly-available, high-scale, secure, mission critical native cloud enterprise SaaS applications in Java, Go or Python.</li>
<li>Knowledge of architectural patterns and archetypes for building mature services, especially for distributed and multi-regional deployments.</li>
<li>Excellent abilities in high level service design - modeling interfaces and internals in a proper modular, composable, and extensible way.</li>
<li>Experience with AWS, GCP or other public cloud infrastructure platforms. Multi-region deployment is a plus.</li>
<li>Experience building distributed systems at scale based on microservices architecture.</li>
<li>Extensive experience with REST API design and implementation.</li>
<li>Experience working with data architecture such as Clickhouse, Lakehouse, Snowflake</li>
<li>Experience with SQL/NoSQL databases and caches.</li>
<li>Familiarity with streaming systems such as Kafka, Flink, Spark</li>
<li>Experience with container technologies - Docker/Kubernetes.</li>
<li>Experience implementing modern automated testing and CI/CD pipelines. Develop, Test, Deploy your code.</li>
<li>Strong expertise in solving complex production issues</li>
</ul>
