## 🤖 AI & Automation Concepts

> [!ABSTRACT] 1. Multi-Platform Syndication Score Boost
> - **Concept:** When a job post is detected on multiple channels (e.g. RSS + Reddit + LinkedIn), boost its priority score by `+0.05` per extra channel.
> - **Value:** Multi-platform posting indicates urgent hiring intent and higher commercial budget.

---

> [!EXAMPLE] 2. Live Outreach Assistant (Telegram Bot Integration)
> - **Concept:** When a high-score lead (`>= 98.0`) is posted, the Telegram Bot sends the CRM report with interactive inline buttons:
>   - `[Draft Pitch]` — Generates personalized email pitch.
>   - `[Export to Notion]` — Pushes lead to Notion CRM database.
>   - `[Ignore Company]` — Adds domain to `you.txt` blocklist.

---

> [!NOTE] 3. Local LLM Fallback (Ollama Integration)
> - **Concept:** For 100% privacy and zero API costs, allow falling back to local Ollama (Llama 3 / Mistral) when offline or rate-limited.

---

## 📈 Commercial & Marketing Positioning

> [!IMPORTANT] Pitching Archangel vs. Raw Claude/ChatGPT
> - **Claude:** Passive Q&A tool that forgets context after every session, cannot crawl web streams 24/7, and burns tokens on raw text.
> - **Archangel:** Autonomous revenue engine running 300+ parallel workers, zero-token regex filtering, persistent SQLite WAL storage, and structured budget extraction.
