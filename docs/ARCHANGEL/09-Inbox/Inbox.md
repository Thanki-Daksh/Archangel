## 📝 Raw Ideas & Observations
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Extend `--budget` and `BudgetNormalizer` to handle Euro (`€`), British Pound (`£`), and Canadian Dollar (`CAD`).
- **Telegram Command Controls:** Allow triggering `/swarm`, `/leads`, and `/status` directly inside the Telegram Bot chat.
- **Obsidian Vault Sync Automation:** Auto-export top 5% priority CRM lead reports directly into `ARCHANGEL/Leads/` folder inside Obsidian.
